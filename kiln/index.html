What can an old kiln teach us about designing new systems?

A century-old approach to tile firing holds remarkable ideas about heat recovery, continuous processes and resource efficiency.

By unpacking the Hoffmann kiln as a computational system, this project explores the engineering principles behind its operation, revealing how an ingenious approach to organising heat, time and resources could inspire new ways of thinking about contemporary industrial processes.
