01 · Three questions

Three different questions, never one scale

How a fact is known, how strong a reading is, and how real an application is are separate questions. Only the first is marked with a letter. The other two appear only where they apply, so that a mark never turns into a score of how true something is.

03 · Fieldwork

What was seen, and what was told

Everything marked F on this site, in one list. "Field" covers two different things: what was seen or counted on site, and what people said. Where the research does not say which, neither does this page.

Limits: one site, one season, a few conversations. No instruments were used, and nothing was weighed or timed. The photographs on the kiln page show other kilns.
